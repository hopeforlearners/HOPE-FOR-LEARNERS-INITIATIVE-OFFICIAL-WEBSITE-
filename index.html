Our Mission

To improve access to quality education and create opportunities for vulnerable learners.

Our vision

A society where every learner has equal access to education,hope,dignity and education to achieve their full potential.

Our Objectives

  • Support vulnerable learners with school uniforms and educational materials.
  • Promote equal access to quality education for all children.
  • Improve school attendance, confidence, and academic performance.
  • Provide mentorship and life skills training to young people.
  • Build partnerships with schools, communities, donors, and volunteers.
  • Promote transparency, accountability, and sustainable community development.

Our Founder

Hope for Learners Initiative (HLI) was founded by Judah Maluki, a Community Development graduate from Moi University. Inspired by his experience in community development and working with vulnerable communities, he established HLI to ensure that every child has an equal opportunity to access quality education. His vision is to restore dignity to vulnerable learners by providing school uniforms, educational support, mentorship, and building strong partnerships that create lasting change in communities.

the first project of Hope for Learners Initiative (HLI)is the school uniform support programme. The project aims at supporting 100 vulnerable learners by providing complete school uniforms, school shoes and socks to improve school attendance, confidence and academic performance. The budget below outlines the estimated costs required to successfully implement this project.The total budget is KSH450,000.
